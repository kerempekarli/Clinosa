The problem

The admin problem isn't inside one screen any more.

Your PMS holds the medical record. But the work that surrounds it — chasing approvals, payments, follow-ups, portal messages — has spread across calls, emails, and browser tabs. The team feels stretched, and clients fall through gaps.

See how Clinosa addresses this
  • 01
    Estimate approvals get lost in calls, emails, and reminders.
    Owners want clarity. Your team wants the green light. Without one channel, both wait.
  • 02
    Payments need manual chasing across phone, email, and reception.
    Outstanding invoices pile up in places nobody is checking.
  • 03
    Follow-ups depend on memory and luck.
    Post-op check-ins, vaccine reminders, and lab result messages need a system, not a sticky note.
  • 04
    Reception teams lack a clear daily attention queue.
    "What's most important right now?" deserves a real answer, not twenty-four unread tabs.
  • 05
    Client-side activity is hard to see across the clinic.
    Who saw what, when? Without one timeline, handovers turn into guesswork.

Common questions
Do I need to replace my PMS?
No. Clinosa is the operational layer around your existing PMS. You can keep your medical record system as-is.
How long is onboarding?
Most clinics go live in 3–5 working days. We import owner contacts, set up templates, and train the team.
What about the design partner discount?
Design partner clinics use Clinosa at no charge for the duration of the programme — typically two quarters.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Annual contracts can be paused on Network if the practice needs it.
Where is my data hosted?
United Kingdom (London region), encrypted at rest and in transit. Data residency can be customised on Network.
What payment methods do you support?
Card and Direct Debit for monthly billing. BACS and invoice for Network plans. We don't take payment from owners directly.
