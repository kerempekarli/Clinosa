Platform architecture

From daily clinic workflows to a complete Clinic OS.

tryclinosa starts where clinics feel the most pressure: client requests, estimates, payments, follow-ups, prescriptions, and daily attention. From there, the platform expands into intelligence, automation, AI assistance, integrations, and full practice operations.

L1 Client-facing layer

Client Portal

The client-facing layer for requests, approvals, payments, forms, and communication.

Give clients one place to handle pet-related workflows: intake, consent, estimate approvals, invoice payments, secure messages, lab-result sharing, communication preferences, and follow-up actions. The goal is to reduce front-desk pressure while giving clinics a cleaner client experience.

L2 Intelligence layer

tryclinosa Intelligence

The operational visibility layer for what needs attention today.

tryclinosa Intelligence turns clinic activity into attention queues, rankings, dashboards, payment health, schedule pressure, clinical queues, workload signals, and workflow summaries. It helps the team see what is waiting, what is risky, and what should be handled next.

L3 Automation layer

Automations

The rule engine that follows up when the clinic would normally have to remember.

Automations help trigger reminders, payment follow-ups, estimate nudges, no-show recovery, post-visit follow-ups, prescription request routing, and staff tasks. The system is designed so clinics can control what runs, what requires review, and what should stay manual.

L4 Assistant layer

Ask tryclinosa

The role-aware assistant for asking questions about clinic operations.

Ask tryclinosa lets teams ask questions about their own clinic data: overdue payments, pending estimates, prescription queues, client follow-ups, workload, and daily priorities. It respects role permissions and turns operational data into usable answers.

L5 AI assistance layer

AI Tools

Review-first AI tools for drafting, explaining, summarising, and triaging.

AI tools help prepare follow-up drafts, discharge explanations, intake summaries, prescription responses, client-friendly explanations, and operational summaries. The principle is review-first: AI helps prepare the work, but the clinic team stays in control.

L6 Practice operations layer

Core Practice Management

The deeper practice-management layer tryclinosa can expand into over time.

tryclinosa is designed to sit alongside an existing PMS as an operational workflow layer, while gradually deepening into broader practice-management capabilities: records, migration, accounting, lab workflows, insurance workflows, reporting, integrations, and multi-location operations.

The arc

tryclinosa starts as the workflow layer clinics can adopt quickly, then grows into the operating system that connects the entire practice.

Common questions
Do I need to replace my PMS?
No. tryclinosa is the operational layer around your existing PMS. You can keep your medical record system as-is.
How long is onboarding?
We onboard design partner clinics one at a time: we import owner contacts, set up templates, and train the team with you.
What about the design partner discount?
Design partner clinics use tryclinosa at no charge for the duration of the programme — typically two quarters.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time.
Where is my data hosted?
In the United Kingdom, encrypted at rest and in transit.
What payment methods do you support?
Card and Direct Debit for monthly billing. BACS and invoice for Network plans. We don't take payment from owners directly.
