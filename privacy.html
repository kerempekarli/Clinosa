Website privacy notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This notice explains how tryclinosa handles personal data that you share with us through this website — for example when you ask for a demo or apply to the design partner programme. Clinic data processed inside the tryclinosa product is covered separately by a data processing agreement with each clinic.

Who we are

tryclinosa is an early-stage software team based in Samut Sakhon, Thailand, building an operations layer for veterinary clinics. For any privacy question, contact us at kerem@tryclinosa.com.

What we collect

  • Details you send us: your name, role, work email, clinic name, number of vets and current practice management system.
  • Anything else you choose to include in emails or calls with us.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a demo or call.
  • To run the design partner programme with clinics that join it.

We rely on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries you send us, or on steps you ask us to take before entering into an agreement.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or share it for marketing. It is only handled by the email and productivity providers we use to run the company, under their own data protection terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we're in conversation with you, and delete them on request or when they're no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it, by emailing kerem@tryclinosa.com. If you're in the UK and unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
